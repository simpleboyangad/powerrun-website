Battery Connectors & Terminals

ESS terminals and cables, SB50 to SB350 connectors, XT60, IEC and e-rickshaw battery connectors - in handy packs.

A good connector keeps a battery pack safe at full current: it must be rated above the pack's highest load, crimped or bolted firmly and matched to the plug on the other side. PowerRun stocks the connectors pack builders, e-rickshaw and inverter battery makers use most.

The range covers ESS battery connectors and 200 A terminals, interconnect cables with SC35-6 lugs, SB50, SB75, SB120, SB175 and SB350 connectors, XT60 pairs, IEC charging plugs and Chakori and OKW sockets. Small parts come in packs of 2, 5 or 10; the pack size is in every product name.

How to choose

Match the current

Choose a connector rated above the highest continuous current of your pack - SB and XT numbers roughly follow their current class (SB50, SB120, SB175, SB350; XT60).

Match the other side

A plug only mates with its own type. Replacing a socket on an e-rickshaw or charger? Pick the same type (Chakori, OKW, D-type or IEC) that is already fitted.

ESS and inverter batteries

Rack and inverter batteries use panel-mount ESS terminals and short 4 AWG cables with lugs to link modules.

Most connectors cost only a few rupees each, so each listing is a pack (2, 5 or 10 pieces) to keep delivery worthwhile. The pack size is in the product name and the price is for the whole pack. Need a different quantity? Ask us on WhatsApp.

ज़्यादातर कनेक्टर कुछ ही रुपये के होते हैं, इसलिए हर लिस्टिंग एक पैक है (2, 5 या 10 पीस) ताकि डिलीवरी का खर्च निकल सके। पैक साइज़ प्रोडक्ट के नाम में लिखा है और कीमत पूरे पैक की है। अलग मात्रा चाहिए तो WhatsApp पर पूछें।

Which connector do I need?मुझे कौन सा कनेक्टर चाहिए?

Pick a connector rated above the highest current of your pack: SB50 and XT60 for small packs and chargers, SB120 to SB350 or ESS terminals for inverter and ESS batteries. E-rickshaw packs commonly use Chakori or OKW sockets - match the one already on your vehicle or charger.

ऐसा कनेक्टर चुनें जिसकी रेटिंग आपकी बैटरी के सबसे ज़्यादा करंट से ऊपर हो: छोटे पैक और चार्जर के लिए SB50 और XT60, इन्वर्टर और ESS बैटरी के लिए SB120 से SB350 या ESS टर्मिनल। ई-रिक्शा पैक में अक्सर चकोरी या OKW सॉकेट लगते हैं - जो आपकी गाड़ी या चार्जर में पहले से लगा है, वही लें।
