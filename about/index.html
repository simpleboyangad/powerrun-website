About PowerRun Industries

Powering today, sustaining tomorrow.

Who we are

PowerRun Industries builds and supplies high-performance energy products for Indian homes, businesses and electric mobility: LiFePO4 lithium battery packs, grid-interactive hybrid inverters, high-efficiency solar modules and e-rickshaw battery systems.

What we stand for

Every PowerRun product is built around three ideas — safe chemistry, intelligent protection and a long, predictable service life. Our lithium packs ship with an integrated smart BMS, our inverters carry built-in MPPT solar charging, and every unit is tested before dispatch.

Applications we serve

Residential backup and solar storage, commercial and industrial energy systems, UPS and power backup, and electric mobility including e-rickshaw and e-loader fleets.

Support you can reach

Warranty registration, service requests and order tracking are all handled directly on this website, and our team is reachable on WhatsApp and phone for sizing and technical help.
