Hybrid Solar Inverters

Solar, battery and grid power in one unit - 3.6 kW to 12 kW, pure sine wave output and built-in MPPT solar charging.

A PowerRun hybrid inverter combines an inverter, an MPPT solar charge controller and a battery charger in one box. It runs your load from solar first, stores the surplus in your battery and draws on the grid only when it has to, switching between sources in 10 ms so computers, Wi-Fi and appliances keep running through a power cut.

The range covers 3.6 kW to 12 kW. The 3.6 kW and 4.2 kW models run on a 24 V battery bank; the 6.2 kW and larger models run on 48 V. All are pure sine wave with up to 98% efficiency, an LCD display and optional Wi-Fi monitoring, and carry a 5-year warranty.

How to choose

Size it by your load

Add up the wattage of everything that has to run at the same time and choose an inverter whose rated output is above that total. Peak power is twice the rated output, which covers the start-up surge of motors, pumps and compressors.

Match the battery voltage

3.6 kW and 4.2 kW models use a 24 V battery - pair them with PowerRun 25.6 V lithium batteries. 6.2 kW, 8.2 kW, 10.2 kW and 12 kW models use 48 V - pair them with PowerRun 51.2 V lithium batteries.

Check the solar input

The maximum PV input decides how many panels you can connect: 5,000 W on the 3.6 kW model up to 2 x 8,500 W across the dual MPPT inputs of the 10.2 kW and 12 kW models.

Plan for growth

The 8.2 kW, 10.2 kW and 12 kW models support parallel operation, so you can add a second unit later instead of replacing the first.
