Har product ke liye: **IndiaMART naam, Amazon title, 5 bullet points, description aur specs**. Grey box mein jo likha hai, use seedha copy karke paste kar dein.

> Ye file sirf aapke liye hai: `docs/` folder website par nahi dikhta.
> Prices 9 Oct 2026 ki website ki prices hain (GST included). Price badlein to listing mein bhi badlein.
> Business details (naam, pata, GST, phone) ke liye `docs/directory-listings.md` dekhein: har jagah bilkul same details daalein.

## Pehle ye padh lein

**IndiaMART**
1. Free listing se shuru karein. Pehle 10 products daalein: 3.6kW, 6.2kW inverter, 51.2V 100Ah, 51.2V 200Ah LFP, 25.6V 100Ah LFP, 51.2V 110Ah aur 150Ah e-rickshaw, 535W, 550W panel, aur 1 BMS group (neeche hai).
2. Har product mein kam se kam **3 photo** lagayein: `photos/listing/` folder mein ready hain. Price zaroor daalein: price wali listing par zyada buyer enquiry karte hain.
3. Enquiry aate hi **15 minute ke andar** call/WhatsApp karein. IndiaMART fast reply karne waale seller ko upar dikhata hai. Har lead ko admin panel mein **Leads** mein source "indiamart" ke saath daalein.
4. "BuyLeads" mein roz 5-10 leads dekhein (e-rickshaw battery, lithium battery, solar inverter search karke).

**Amazon**
1. Lithium battery Amazon par **Dangerous Goods (Class 9)** maani jaati hai. Listing ke saath **SDS (Safety Data Sheet)** aur **UN38.3 test report** maangte hain: ye apne cell/battery supplier se lein. Iske bina listing ruk jaati hai.
2. Inverter aur solar panel ke liye BIS (CRS) registration number maang sakte hain. Supplier se BIS certificate ki copy pehle se rakh lein.
3. Bade/bhaari products (solar panel, 51.2V 200Ah+ battery) ke liye **Easy Ship** ki jagah **Self Ship** rakhein, warna shipping charge mein loss hoga.
4. Shuru mein Amazon par chhote aur halke products rakhein: **BMS, balancer, display, e-scooty battery, 25.6V 100Ah**.
5. Brand name "PowerRun" ke liye **Amazon Brand Registry** baad mein karein (trademark application number chahiye).

**Dono jagah:** title mein voltage, Ah/kW aur "LiFePO4" zaroor rakhein. Customer yahi search karta hai.
