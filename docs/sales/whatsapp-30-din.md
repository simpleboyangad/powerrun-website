# WhatsApp: 30 din ka plan (Status + Broadcast)

> Ye file sirf aapke liye hai: `docs/` folder website par nahi dikhta.
> Prices 9 Oct 2026 ki website ki prices hain. Price badlein to message mein bhi badlein.

## Kaise chalana hai

- **Roz 1 Status** (subah 9-10 baje): photo/video + neeche wala text. Status 24 ghante dikhta hai aur sab contacts dekhte hain.
- **Hafte mein 2 Broadcast** (Mangal aur Shanivaar, shaam 7-8 baje): neeche "📢 Broadcast" wale din. Broadcast sirf unko jaata hai jinke phone mein aapka number save hai, isliye har customer se kahein: *"Number save kar lijiye, offer aate rahenge."*
- **Broadcast list banayein (WhatsApp Business → Broadcast lists):**
  1. *E-rickshaw* (drivers, owners, garages)
  2. *Ghar/Dukaan* (inverter, solar customers)
  3. *Dealer/Mechanic* (electricians, solar installers, battery shops)
- Ek broadcast list mein **256 se zyada** log nahi aa sakte. Zyada ho jayein to list 2 bana lein.
- Photo ke liye `photos/listing/` folder ki photos, ya apni dukaan/installation ki asli photo lein. **Asli photo hamesha zyada chalti hai.**
- Koi reply kare to **usi din** jawab dein, aur admin panel → **Leads** mein daal dein (source: whatsapp).
- Messages mein `[ ]` wali jagah apni jaankari bharein.
