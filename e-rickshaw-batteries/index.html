How to choose

Check your system voltage

PowerRun e-rickshaw packs are 51.2 V, for 48 V / 51.2 V e-rickshaw and e-loader systems. Confirm your controller voltage before ordering - we are happy to check it with you.

Choose capacity by daily use

More amp-hours means more energy per charge and more distance per day. Drivers running long shifts or carrying heavy loads should look at 230 Ah or 314 Ah.

Count the cycles

Cycle life rises with capacity: 4000+ cycles on 110 Ah and 150 Ah packs, 6000+ on 230 Ah and 8000+ on 314 Ah.

Why lithium instead of lead-acid

No water topping, no acid spills and much faster charging, with far more cycles per pack - less downtime for the vehicle.

PowerRun e-rickshaw batteries are 51.2 V packs for 48 V / 51.2 V e-rickshaw and e-loader systems. Send us your vehicle model and controller voltage on WhatsApp and we will confirm the fit before you order.

Lithium packs need a charger made for 51.2 V LiFePO4. Ask us which charger suits the pack you choose.
