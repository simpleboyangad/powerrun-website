Energy and charging

The PR E-Scooty Battery 60V 30Ah stores 1.80 kWh (60 V × 30 Ah). A full charge from empty takes about 2.0 units of electricity from the grid, allowing for about 90% charger efficiency. At its maximum charge current of 15 A it charges from empty in roughly 2 h 12 min; a smaller charger takes proportionally longer (capacity in Ah ÷ charger amps). Its BMS allows up to 30 A continuous, which is about 1.8 kW at the motor controller.

Charging time by charger size

Approximate time from empty to full (capacity ÷ charger current, plus about 10% for the final balancing stage), and what a full charge costs at ₹8 a unit:
