The PowerRun 51.2V 200Ah LiFePO4 battery delivers 10.24kWh of usable storage, built to work with 48V hybrid inverters for whole-home backup or solar self-consumption. Backup time on this battery

The PR LFP Battery 51.2V 200Ah stores 10.24 kWh (51.2 V × 200 Ah). Through an inverter about 8.3 kWh reaches your appliances (81% of the stored energy, the same allowance our battery calculator uses), so backup time is about 8294 Wh ÷ the load:

Load runningBackup time
300 W27 h 39 min
500 W16 h 35 min
1.0 kW8 h 18 min
1.5 kW5 h 32 min
2.0 kW4 h 9 min
3.0 kW2 h 46 min
5.0 kW1 h 40 min

यह बैटरी 10.24 kWh स्टोर करती है। इन्वर्टर से लगभग 8.3 kWh उपकरणों तक पहुँचती है, जैसे 1 kW लोड पर लगभग 8 h 18 min बैकअप।

Inverters that take this battery

This is a 48 V class battery (51.2 V nominal), so it pairs with hybrid inverters built for a 48 V DC battery: PR Hybrid Inverter 6.2kW, PR Hybrid Inverter 8.2kW, PR Hybrid Inverter 11kW, PR Hybrid Inverter 12kW. Check the inverter battery setting is on LiFePO4 / lithium before first charge.

Solar to recharge it in a day

In most of India 1 kW of panels makes about 4 units (kWh) on a clear day, so this battery needs about 2.6 kW of panels to refill from empty in one sunny day. Panels come in whole units, so in practice that is 5 panels of 550 W (2.8 kW). Solar running your daytime load at the same time needs more panels on top.
