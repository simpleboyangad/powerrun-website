Backup time on this battery

The PR LFP Battery 25.6V 550Ah stores 14.08 kWh (25.6 V × 550 Ah). Through an inverter about 11.4 kWh reaches your appliances (81% of the stored energy, the same allowance our battery calculator uses), so backup time is about 11405 Wh ÷ the load:

Load runningBackup time
300 W38 h 1 min
500 W22 h 49 min
1.0 kW11 h 24 min
1.5 kW7 h 36 min
2.0 kW5 h 42 min
3.0 kW3 h 48 min
5.0 kW2 h 17 min

यह बैटरी 14.08 kWh स्टोर करती है। इन्वर्टर से लगभग 11.4 kWh उपकरणों तक पहुँचती है, जैसे 1 kW लोड पर लगभग 11 h 24 min बैकअप।

Inverters that take this battery

This is a 24 V class battery (25.6 V nominal), so it pairs with hybrid inverters built for a 24 V DC battery: PR Hybrid Inverter 3.6kW, PR Hybrid Inverter 4.2kW. Check the inverter battery setting is on LiFePO4 / lithium before first charge.

Solar to recharge it in a day

In most of India 1 kW of panels makes about 4 units (kWh) on a clear day, so this battery needs about 3.5 kW of panels to refill from empty in one sunny day. Panels come in whole units, so in practice that is 7 panels of 550 W (3.9 kW). Solar running your daytime load at the same time needs more panels on top.
