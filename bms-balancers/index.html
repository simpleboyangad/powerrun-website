JK BMS & Active Balancers

JK smart BMS from 4S to 32S, ESS and high-voltage BMS, active balancers and SOC displays for lithium battery packs.

A battery management system (BMS) protects a lithium pack from over-charge, over-discharge, over-current and short circuits, and keeps its cells at the same voltage. PowerRun stocks JK BMS boards for pack builders, e-rickshaw and inverter battery makers and repair shops.

The range covers smart and semi-smart BMS from 4S to 32S and 40 A to 300 A, ESS BMS for inverter battery packs, high-voltage master/slave BMS, smart active balancers and SOC displays. Every product page lists the series range, current and balance current.

How to choose

Count your cells in series

LiFePO4 cells are 3.2 V each: 4S = 12.8 V, 8S = 25.6 V, 16S = 51.2 V, 20S = 64 V, 24S = 76.8 V. Pick a BMS whose range includes your series count.

Size the current

The continuous current of the BMS should be at least the highest steady load of your inverter, controller or motor, with some margin.

Pick the balance current

Larger packs and cells that drift apart benefit from higher active balancing (1 A or 2 A instead of 0.4 A or 0.6 A). A separate active balancer can be added to an existing pack.

ESS and high-voltage systems

Inverter battery racks use ESS BMS; stacks above 100 V use a JK master BMS (BCU) with one slave module (BMU) per block.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

अक्सर पूछे जाने वाले सवाल

Which JK BMS do I need?मुझे कौन सा JK BMS चाहिए?

Match two things: the series count of your pack (for example 16S for a 51.2 V LiFePO4 pack) must fall inside the BMS range, and the BMS current must be at least your maximum continuous load. Send us your cell type, series count and current on WhatsApp and we will suggest a model.

दो चीज़ें मिलाएँ: आपकी बैटरी के सेल की संख्या (जैसे 51.2 V LiFePO4 पैक के लिए 16S) BMS की रेंज में होनी चाहिए, और BMS का करंट आपके सबसे ज़्यादा लगातार लोड से कम नहीं होना चाहिए। अपने सेल का टाइप, सीरीज़ और करंट WhatsApp पर भेजें, हम सही मॉडल बता देंगे।

What is the difference between Smart and Semi-Smart JK BMS?स्मार्ट और सेमी-स्मार्ट JK BMS में क्या फ़र्क है?

Both protect the pack and balance the cells actively. Smart models are listed with Bluetooth app monitoring; check each product page for the exact features before you order.

दोनों बैटरी को सुरक्षित रखते हैं और सेल्स को एक्टिव बैलेंस करते हैं। स्मार्ट मॉडल में ब्लूटूथ ऐप से मॉनिटरिंग मिलती है; ऑर्डर से पहले हर प्रोडक्ट पेज पर पूरे फ़ीचर देख लें।

What does the SKU mean?SKU का क्या मतलब है?

Our SKUs read as series range, current and type. JK-BMS-8S-24S-40A-SEMI is a semi-smart BMS for 8S to 24S packs at 40 A; SMART means 0.4-0.6 A active balancing, PRO means 1-2 A active balancing and ESS is made for inverter battery packs.

हमारे SKU में सीरीज़ रेंज, करंट और टाइप लिखा होता है। JK-BMS-8S-24S-40A-SEMI का मतलब 8S से 24S पैक के लिए 40 A का सेमी-स्मार्ट BMS; SMART यानी 0.4-0.6 A एक्टिव बैलेंसिंग, PRO यानी 1-2 A एक्टिव बैलेंसिंग, और ESS इन्वर्टर बैटरी पैक के लिए।
