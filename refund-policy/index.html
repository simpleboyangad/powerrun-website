Return, Replacement & Refund Policy

What happens if your product arrives damaged, defective or incorrect.

Last updated: 19 September 2026

7-day replacement

If your product arrives damaged in transit, defective, or different from what you ordered, tell us within 7 days of delivery and we will replace it.

  • Contact us on +91 87003 07676 (call or WhatsApp) or service@powerrun.in with your order number.
  • Share clear photos or a short video of the product, the label/serial number and the packaging.
  • Keep the product with its original box, accessories and invoice until the replacement is arranged.
  • After we verify the issue, we arrange pickup and send a replacement at no cost to you.

Not eligible for return or replacement

  • A change of mind, or a product that is working as described.
  • Damage caused by incorrect installation, wiring, overloading, water or physical impact.
  • Products that have been opened up, modified or repaired by someone else.
  • Requests made more than 7 days after delivery - these are handled under the product warranty instead.

After 7 days: warranty

Problems that appear later are covered by the product warranty shown on the product page. Raise a service request and our team will guide you.

Cancellations

You can cancel free of charge before the order is dispatched. Once dispatched, the order can no longer be cancelled; the replacement rules above apply after delivery.

Refunds

A refund is issued when an order is cancelled before dispatch, when we cannot fulfil it, or when a replacement is not possible. Refunds are made to the original payment method within 5–7 working days of approval. Bank processing may take a little longer to show in your account.
