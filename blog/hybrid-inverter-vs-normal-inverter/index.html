Hybrid Inverter vs Normal Inverter: Which One Should You Buy?

6 Oct 2026 · 4 min read

A normal inverter and a hybrid inverter both keep the lights on during a power cut. The difference is what they do the rest of the day. A normal inverter only waits for the grid to fail. A hybrid inverter runs your home on sunlight every day and cuts your electricity bill as well.

What a normal inverter does

A normal (or "home UPS") inverter has two jobs: charge a battery from the grid, and power your home from that battery when the grid goes off. It has no solar input. Every unit of electricity it supplies was bought from the grid first, so it saves you nothing on your bill. In fact, charging and discharging a battery wastes some energy, so it adds a little to it.

What a hybrid inverter does

A hybrid inverter is three machines in one box: an inverter, a solar charge controller (MPPT) and a battery charger. During the day it works in this order:

  • Solar first: your appliances run on power from the panels.
  • Surplus to the battery: extra solar charges the battery for the evening.
  • Grid only when needed: the grid tops up when solar and battery are not enough, for example at night or on a cloudy day.

When the grid fails, it switches to battery and solar automatically. PowerRun hybrid inverters switch in 10 ms, fast enough that computers and Wi-Fi routers do not restart.

Can I add solar panels to my normal inverter?

Some "solar-ready" normal inverters accept a small solar charge controller, but the solar only charges the battery. Your home still runs on grid power during the day, so the bill barely moves. If saving on the bill is the goal, a hybrid inverter is the right tool.

Which one is right for you?

Choose a normal inverter if

  • You only need a few lights and fans during short power cuts.
  • You rent and cannot put panels on the roof.
  • Your budget is very tight right now.

Choose a hybrid inverter if

  • Your monthly bill is above Rs 1,500-2,000 and you have roof space for panels.
  • You want an AC, water pump or fridge to keep running during long cuts.
  • Power cuts in your area last several hours.
  • You run a shop, clinic, office or small factory where downtime costs money.

Picking the right size

PowerRun hybrid inverters come in six sizes. The 3.6 kW and 4.2 kW models run on 24 V lithium batteries and suit most homes. The 6.2 kW and larger models run on 48 V and suit bigger homes and businesses. Our guide on what size solar inverter you need walks through the sizing with worked examples, and lithium vs lead-acid explains why lithium is the better battery for a hybrid system.

The bottom line

A normal inverter is a backup device. A hybrid inverter is a backup device that also earns its cost back by cutting your bill every month. If you have a roof and a bill worth reducing, the hybrid inverter is the better long-term buy.
