Looking for a 3kW, 5kW or 10kW hybrid inverter?

Most people search by a round number, but inverter ratings rarely are. Here is how the PowerRun range lines up:

  • 3 kW / 3.5 kVA class: the 3.6 kW model. Runs lights, fans, TV, fridge and a small motor, with room to spare.
  • 4-5 kW class on a 24 V battery: the 4.2 kW model.
  • 5 kW class on a 48 V battery: the 6.2 kW model. It takes up to 8,500 W of panels, so a 5 kW rooftop array fits comfortably with space to add more.
  • 8 kW class: the 8.2 kW model, with two solar inputs and parallel operation.
  • 10 kW class: the 11 kW or 12 kW model, for larger homes, shops and small businesses.

Not sure what size you need? Our guide What Size Solar Inverter Do I Need? walks through it with worked examples, or try the solar calculator.

24 V or 48 V: why battery voltage matters

The 3.6 kW and 4.2 kW models run on a 24 V battery; the 6.2 kW and above run on 48 V. A 48 V system carries the same power at half the current, so cables run cooler and the inverter can deliver more. If you expect to add AC, a motor pump or more panels later, a 48 V setup leaves more room to grow. Match the battery to the inverter: PowerRun LiFePO4 batteries come in both 25.6 V (24 V class) and 51.2 V (48 V class).

What does a full system cost?

The inverter is only one part. A typical 5 kW home system also needs panels and a battery. Using PowerRun prices, a 6.2 kW inverter with ten 550 W panels and a 5.12 kWh lithium battery comes to about ₹1,85,000 for the equipment, before structure, wiring and installation. Our 5kW solar system cost guide breaks this down line by line.

Five things to check before you buy

  1. Rated output, not peak. Compare the continuous rating; peak (VA) only covers motor start-up surges.
  2. Maximum solar input. This decides how many panels you can connect, now and later.
  3. Battery voltage and type. Make sure the inverter supports a lithium (LiFePO4) battery at your chosen voltage.
  4. Pure sine wave output. Needed for fridges, ACs, pumps and electronics to run safely.
  5. Warranty and service. Check who services it in your area. PowerRun inverters carry a 2-year warranty with service support.
