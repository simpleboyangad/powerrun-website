Which gives the best value?

Compare batteries on price per kWh, not price per Ah: a 48 V 100 Ah and a 24 V 200 Ah both store 5.12 kWh. On that basis the 48 V packs generally work out cheaper - about ₹11,000 to ₹12,500 per kWh, against ₹12,300 to ₹15,400 per kWh for the 24 V packs. The best value in the range is the 48 V 600 Ah at about ₹11,084 per kWh, followed by the 48 V 280 Ah at about ₹11,196.

Is lithium worth it over lead-acid?

A tubular lead-acid battery is cheaper to buy, but you can safely use only about half its capacity, it needs water topping and it wears out after far fewer cycles. A LiFePO4 battery gives about 80% usable capacity, needs no maintenance, charges faster from solar and is rated for 6000+ cycles. See the full comparison in Lithium vs Lead-Acid Battery for Inverters.

How to choose the right lithium inverter battery

  1. Match the voltage to your inverter. A 24 V inverter needs a 25.6 V battery; a 48 V inverter needs a 51.2 V battery.
  2. Size it by your load and backup hours. Battery kWh needed = load (kW) × hours ÷ 0.72.
  3. Check the BMS and warranty. PowerRun packs have an integrated smart BMS and a 5-year warranty.
  4. Leave room for solar. With a hybrid inverter, a bigger battery stores more of the day's solar for the night.
