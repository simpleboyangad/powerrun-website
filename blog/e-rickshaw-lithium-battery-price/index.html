E-rickshaw battery price: lithium vs lead-acid

A lead-acid set is cheaper on the first day, but it needs regular water topping, charges slowly and wears out far sooner, so you pay for a new set again and again. A LiFePO4 pack is maintenance-free, charges faster and is rated for thousands of cycles, so the vehicle spends more days earning and the battery is replaced far less often. Our e-rickshaw lithium battery buying guide goes through the switch step by step.

Three checks before you buy

  1. Voltage. PowerRun packs are 51.2 V, for 48 V / 51.2 V e-rickshaws and e-loaders. Check your controller voltage.
  2. Charger. A lithium pack needs a 51.2 V LiFePO4 charger. An old lead-acid charger uses the wrong charging profile.
  3. Space. Measure the battery box in your vehicle before ordering.
