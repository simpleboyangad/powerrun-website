Lithium & Lead-Acid Battery Chargers

60 V and 72 V lead-acid chargers for e-rickshaws and e-scooters, and 29.2 V / 42 V lithium chargers for small LiFePO4 and Li-ion packs.

A battery lasts longest on a charger made for its chemistry and cell count. Lithium packs need a charger that stops at the right voltage - 3.65 V per cell for LiFePO4 and 4.2 V per cell for Li-ion - while lead-acid batteries need a lead-acid charger of the same voltage.

The range covers 60 V and 72 V lead-acid chargers from GRAPHY, SUN6, QT7 Pulse and ELOW9, and 3 A / 5 A lithium chargers for 24 V LiFePO4 (29.2 V) and 36 V Li-ion (42 V) packs. Every product page says which battery it suits; for other voltages, ask us on WhatsApp.

How to choose

Chemistry first

LiFePO4, Li-ion and lead-acid each need their own charger. Never charge a lithium pack on a lead-acid charger.

Then the voltage

LiFePO4: 8S = 29.2 V, 16S = 58.4 V, 19S = 69.4 V, 20S = 73 V. Li-ion: 10S = 42 V, 13S = 54.6 V, 16S = 67.2 V. Lead-acid: 60 V (5 x 12 V) or 72 V (6 x 12 V).

Then the current

More amps charge faster. Small packs and scooter batteries usually use 3-5 A; larger e-rickshaw packs need a higher-current charger - ask us for one that suits your pack.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

अक्सर पूछे जाने वाले सवाल

Which charger does my battery need?मेरी बैटरी के लिए कौन सा चार्जर चाहिए?

Match the chemistry and the cell count. LiFePO4 packs charge to 3.65 V per cell (16S = 58.4 V, 20S = 73 V); Li-ion packs to 4.2 V per cell (13S = 54.6 V, 16S = 67.2 V). Lead-acid chargers are sold by battery voltage (60 V = 5 x 12 V). Every product page lists the pack it suits.

केमिस्ट्री और सेल की संख्या मिलाएँ। LiFePO4 पैक 3.65 V प्रति सेल तक चार्ज होता है (16S = 58.4 V, 20S = 73 V); Li-ion पैक 4.2 V प्रति सेल तक (13S = 54.6 V, 16S = 67.2 V)। लेड-एसिड चार्जर बैटरी वोल्टेज से चुने जाते हैं (60 V = 5 x 12 V)। हर प्रोडक्ट पेज पर लिखा है कि वह किस पैक के लिए है।

Can I use a lead-acid charger on a lithium battery?क्या लेड-एसिड चार्जर से लिथियम बैटरी चार्ज कर सकते हैं?

No. Lithium batteries need a charger made for their chemistry and cell count; a lead-acid or wrong-voltage charger can damage the pack. When you switch an e-rickshaw from lead-acid to lithium, change the charger too.

नहीं। लिथियम बैटरी के लिए उसकी केमिस्ट्री और सेल की संख्या वाला चार्जर ही चाहिए; लेड-एसिड या ग़लत वोल्टेज का चार्जर बैटरी को ख़राब कर सकता है। ई-रिक्शा में लेड-एसिड से लिथियम बदलें तो चार्जर भी बदलें।
