Shipping Policy

Delivery charges, timelines and what to check when your order arrives.

Last updated: 19 September 2026

Where we deliver

We deliver across India. For a few remote pin codes, we may confirm availability with you before dispatch.

Shipping charges

Shipping is free on orders placed on powerrun.in. If this ever changes, the charge will be shown clearly at checkout before you place the order.

Delivery time

  • Dispatch: within 1–2 working days after the order is confirmed.
  • Delivery: usually 3–7 working days after dispatch, depending on your location.
  • Remote areas, public holidays and weather can add a few days. We will keep you informed.

Tracking your order

Use Track Order with your order number and mobile number, or check My Orders if you have an account.

When your order arrives

  • Check that the box is sealed and undamaged before accepting it.
  • If the box is damaged, note it on the courier’s delivery slip and take photos.
  • Report any transit damage within 7 days under our replacement policy.

Heavy items

Batteries and inverters are heavy. Delivery is to the ground floor or kerbside of the given address unless agreed otherwise. Installation is not included in delivery.
